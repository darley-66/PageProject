Use Case 4

Real Estate Price Prediction

Use Case Name
House Price Prediction

A regression model that estimates the selling price of a house based on its characteristics.

Problem Context

Real estate agents and buyers need accurate price estimates to make informed decisions. Manual appraisal is slow and subjective.

Data That Could Be Used
  • House size (square meters).
  • Number of rooms and bathrooms.
  • Location and neighborhood characteristics.
  • Year of construction and renovations.
What Should the Model Predict?

The model should predict the expected market price of a property.

Type of Machine Learning
Supervised Learning — Regression

Trained with historical data of house sales and their features.

Potential Benefit

Provides accurate price estimates, helps buyers avoid overpaying, and assists sellers in setting competitive prices.
