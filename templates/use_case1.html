Use Case 1

Student Academic Performance Prediction

Name of Use Case
Student Academic Performance Prediction

A Machine Learning system designed to predict students' future academic performance.

Problem Context

Educational institutions have information such as students' grades, attendance and study time. This information can be used to estimate future academic performance and identify students who may need additional support.

Data That Could Be Used
Grades

Previous grades obtained by the student.

Attendance

Percentage of classes attended by the student.

Study Hours

Approximate number of hours dedicated to studying.

What Should the Model Predict?

The model should predict the student's expected final academic grade based on the available data.

Type of Machine Learning
Supervised Learning

Historical data with known academic results can be used to train the model.

Potential Benefit

The system could help educational institutions identify students at risk of low academic performance and provide early academic support.
