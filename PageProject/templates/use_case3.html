Use Case 3

Social Media Sentiment Analysis

Use Case Name
Sentiment Analysis of Social Media Posts

A system that classifies user comments or tweets as positive, negative, or neutral.

Problem Context

Companies and organizations need to understand public opinion about their products or services. Manual review of thousands of posts is impractical.

Data That Could Be Used
  • Text of tweets, comments, or reviews.
  • Hashtags and emojis used.
  • Metadata such as posting time or location.
What Should the Model Predict?

The model should classify each post into categories: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

Type of Machine Learning
Supervised Learning — Text Classification

Trained with labeled datasets of posts tagged with sentiment categories.

Potential Benefit

Helps companies monitor brand reputation, improve customer service, and detect crises early.
